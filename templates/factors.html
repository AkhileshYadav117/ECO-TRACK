🔍 Emission Factor Explorer

Understand how EcoTrack converts your activity data into estimated carbon emissions.

📐 Calculation Methodology
Activity × Emission Factor = Estimated CO₂e

EcoTrack estimates carbon emissions by multiplying user-reported activity quantities by documented emission factors. Results are estimates — not direct physical measurements of greenhouse gas emissions. Actual emissions may vary based on geography, technology, and local energy mix.

Sources used in this project: DEFRA 2023  ·  IPCC AR6  ·  CEA India 2023  ·  MoEFCC India
